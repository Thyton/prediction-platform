METHOD

Market-aware, not opinion

We start from the de-vigged market price, then claim an edge only where our learned team ratings disagree by a real margin.

INTEGRITY

Thin markets flagged

One book quoting, or a lopsided price, means less scrutiny on a match. Those games get a warning instead of a silent tip.

LEARNING

Ratings move daily

Every settled result nudges the ratings of both teams. The model is retrained on finals each cycle, not left stale.
